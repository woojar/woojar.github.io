December 25, 2023 2 min read #learning Learning

I was born in a countryside village, and grew up there. To broaden my view, I think it’s an efficient way to learn something from the interview to the famous people such Elon Musk, Jeff Bezos etc.

Elon MuskJeff BezosYouTube interview by Lex Fridman Jeff is a really determined man who invented Amazon from the scratch, then created Blue Origin which is another high-tech company in the space industry similar with SpaceX.

  • Love physics more than CS (computer science), but have self-awareness not to be good at physics

  • Engineering mindset

  • one way door and two ways door strategies to make decision

  • the encouragement to be a first person to take the space cargo

  • sustainable resources on lunar surface, trying to land human into the moon and manufacture the energy there

  • true seeking methods and culture

  • quickly make decision in an organization is to escalate it instead of being one peer exhausted first, need the organization culture support it (similar Elon’s companies ???)
